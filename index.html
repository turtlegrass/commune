About Commune

Discipline and relaxation, under one roof.

Commune began with a simple idea: the best work happens when people feel at home, and the best rest happens when the work is actually done. So we built a space that holds both. It's quiet enough to focus, warm enough to stay.

In the heart of Siem Reap, our members live, work, and gather without ever leaving the block. Temples and slow mornings are a bike ride away; deep work and good company are down the hall.
